# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students, professionals, families, and local visitors looking for a comfortable cafe in Angeles City, Pampanga. They want to quickly understand the cafe's atmosphere, menu, hours, and contact details.

## Product Purpose

Brew Haven Cafe is a neighborhood coffee shop website. Its job is to present the cafe as welcoming and dependable, show the menu clearly, and make it easy for visitors to plan a visit or contact the shop.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, and neighborly. The brand should feel like a calm local cafe with handcrafted drinks, familiar service, and enough polish to feel intentional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate restaurant pages, stock-heavy coffee templates, unreadable low-contrast brown-on-cream layouts, and decorative effects that distract from menu and visit details.

## Design Principles

- Make the cafe feel specific to a local neighborhood, not like a generic coffee chain.
- Use real drink imagery and practical details so visitors can decide quickly.
- Keep the navigation clear across separate Home, About, Menu, and Contact pages.
- Preserve the original assignment's approachable static-site structure while improving hierarchy and responsiveness.
- Keep motion subtle, fast, and respectful of reduced-motion settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, semantic HTML, descriptive image alt text, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
